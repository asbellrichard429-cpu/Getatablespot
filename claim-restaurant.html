Common Questions
FAQ
Is the Starter plan really free? +
Yes, completely. No credit card, no time limit. You can claim your listing, set your wait time, and start appearing in local searches for free. Upgrade to Growth or Premium whenever you want more visibility.
Do you charge per booking or per cover? +
No. Never. We charge a flat monthly subscription on paid plans — the same whether we send you 10 diners or 1,000. Every walk-in we drive to you is free.
What is the 7-day free trial on paid plans? +
Growth and Premium start with a 7-day free trial. Your card is not charged until day 8. Cancel anytime before that — no questions asked, no charge.
How do I update my wait time? +
Log in to your dashboard from any phone or computer. Update your wait time in one tap — it shows up in real-time on GetATableSpot for nearby diners. Premium plan includes SMS updates so you don't even need to open the app.
What's the difference between Growth and Premium? +
Growth ($49/mo) gives you featured placement in search results and priority ranking — our data shows 2–3× more visibility during slow hours compared to the free plan. Premium ($149/mo) adds POS integration (coming soon), SMS wait-time updates via Twilio, direct diner messaging, and dedicated support.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard or by emailing us. No notice period, no cancellation fees. Your listing stays active until the end of your billing period. The Starter plan is always free and stays active indefinitely.
